Privacy

This page says what the Linkling service stores and for how long. The service is the program a team runs to make short links and to send people on from them. A team that puts Linkling on the internet may put other software in front of it, such as a proxy or a web host, and that software may keep logs of its own. This page cannot speak for those.

If you open a short link

Nothing about you is stored or logged: not your address, your browser, the page you came from or where you are. Opening a link adds one to that link's count for the day, and that is all.

A link's count for the day goes up by one each time the link sends someone on to its target. Chat apps fetch a pasted link once to show a preview, and that fetch counts too: telling it apart from a person would mean reading something about each visitor, which Linkling does not do.

The service writes no access log, and handling a request writes nothing about it to any log.
