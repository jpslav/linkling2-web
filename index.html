Linkling

A small link shortener a team runs for itself. Nobody who clicks a short link is tracked beyond a daily count.

What it is

Linkling turns a long URL into a short one, like links.example.team/q3-plan. Your team runs it itself, with one docker compose up. The team shares one key, and nobody needs an account of their own.

Someone on the team makes a short link from a command-line tool or the API, under a name they choose or a made-up one that is easy to read aloud. A link can expire, or last forever, and it can be deleted. The team sees how many times each link was followed, day by day, on a stats page and from the command line.

What happens when someone clicks

They go straight to the long URL, and nothing is asked of them: no key, no account, no login. The only thing recorded about the click is one more in that link's count for that day. Linkling does not store an IP address, a browser, a referrer or a location.

About this site

This page loads nothing from any other site: no fonts, scripts, analytics or images from elsewhere. The privacy page says exactly what Linkling stores and for how long. The source code is on GitHub.
